CLIENT INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know prior to your luxury visit at ÉCLAT.

How far in advance should I book my bridal consultation?

We recommend reserving your bridal date 3 to 6 months prior to your wedding day. This allows ample time for hair trials, customized skin preparation regimens, and securing your preferred master artist and private suite.

Are your hair coloring products safe for sensitive scalps?

Yes. All ÉCLAT hair couture products are 100% ammonia-free, formaldehyde-free, and enriched with organic plant oils. We conduct a mandatory patch test 24 hours prior to any chemical transformation.

Do you offer private VIP rooms for high-profile clients?

Our salons feature discreet soundproofed VIP chambers with dedicated private entrances, valet parking, and personal concierge staff for total privacy.

What is your appointment cancellation policy?

To respect our master artists' schedules, we request at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Bridal deposits are fully refundable up to 14 days before the event date.
